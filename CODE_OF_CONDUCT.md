# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Zappt to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone,
regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in discussions, issues, and reviews.
- Assume good intent and give constructive, specific feedback.
- Accept that maintainers volunteer their time; be patient.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and PRs that
violate this code, and may block repeat offenders.

To report an issue, open a private report via GitHub or email poysa@proton.me.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
